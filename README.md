## Scope

**As client — art restoration and heritage conservation in Spain**, religious and non-religious
works. The one-page analysis covers what is moving in the sector, the context a consultant would
need, and AI projects already running in the industry. Every claim links to its primary source.

**As consultant — freight logistics and last-mile delivery in Spain.** The deck and brief follow
from a 45-minute discovery session with my pair partner, playing a Madrid-based operator running
pallet freight into a central hub plus B2C last-mile across the metropolitan area.

## The consultant engagement

**Problem.** The client plans each morning on data it cannot trust, and runs each afternoon on a
picture it cannot see. Routes are rebuilt by hand in Excel every day; once the vans leave the hub,
delivery status is reconstructed by phoning drivers.

**Recommended, in order.** Clean addresses with honest delivery windows; an overnight route
proposal the planners review rather than build, aware of both vehicle type and Low Emission Zone
rules; five standardised delivery events so customer service answers at first line.

**Deliberately out of scope.** No ERP replacement, no continuous GPS tracking or driver scoring, no
automated change to a customer commitment, no warehouse automation, no pilot during peak season.

**Next step.** An eight-week pilot in a single Madrid metro zone — the worst residential zone, not
the easiest — with two weeks of baseline and two weeks running in shadow before anything goes live.
